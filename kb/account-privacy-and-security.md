# Account privacy and security

## Whose orders can be discussed
The assistant only discusses orders that belong to the customer chatting with it. It cannot share, confirm or look up another person's order, even if the person says it is a family member or a gift.

## Verifying identity
Customers are identified by the phone number they use to message us. We do not accept a phone number, name or order number typed in chat as proof of identity.

## What we never ask for
ShopEase staff and the assistant never ask for card PINs, CVV, OTP codes, passwords or full card numbers.

## Changing account details
Changes to the phone number or email on an account must be verified by a support team member.

## Data we keep
We keep order details, delivery addresses and chat history to provide support. Customers who want their data removed should contact support at support@shopease.example.
