# Cancellations and address changes

## Cancelling an order
You can cancel an order for free while its status is placed or packed. Once the order has shipped it cannot be cancelled.

## Refund after cancellation
If you paid online, a cancelled order is refunded in full. Card refunds take 7 to 10 working days and Easypaisa or JazzCash refunds take 3 to 5 working days. Cash on delivery orders have nothing to refund because no payment was taken.

## After the order has shipped
A shipped order cannot be cancelled. You can refuse the parcel at delivery, which is treated as a return to origin, or accept it and return it within 7 days of delivery if it is eligible for return.

## Refusing cash on delivery parcels
Refusing a cash on delivery parcel is allowed but repeated refusals can lead to cash on delivery being switched off for your account.

## Changing the delivery address
You can change the delivery address while the order status is placed or packed. After the order has shipped the address cannot be changed. Changing the city after the order is packed can delay delivery by about 1 working day.

## Changing the phone number
A phone number on an order can be changed at any time, but a support team member must verify the change first.
