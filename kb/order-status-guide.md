# Order status guide

## What each status means
Placed means we received your order and it is waiting to be packed. Packed means it is ready for the courier. Shipped means the courier has the parcel and a tracking number is available. Out for delivery means it will arrive today. Delivered means the courier completed the delivery. Cancelled means the order was cancelled and will not arrive. Returned means a return was completed.

## Placed and packed orders
Orders in placed or packed status can still be cancelled or have the address changed.

## Shipped orders
Shipped orders cannot be cancelled or have the address changed. Share the tracking number with the customer so they can follow the parcel.

## Out for delivery
The courier calls before arriving. Ask the customer to keep their phone on and have the exact cash amount ready for cash on delivery orders.

## How long a shipped order should take
See the shipping and delivery policy for delivery times by city. An order that has been shipped for more than 7 working days is late and qualifies for a courier investigation.
