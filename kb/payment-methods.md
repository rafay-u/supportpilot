# Payment methods

## Accepted payment methods
ShopEase accepts cash on delivery, Visa and Mastercard cards, Easypaisa and JazzCash. Bank transfers and instalments are not available.

## Cash on delivery
Pay the courier in cash when the parcel arrives. Please keep the exact amount ready because couriers often do not carry change. If the courier asks for more than the order total, do not pay and contact support.

## Card payments
Cards are charged at checkout. If your card was charged but no order was created, the amount is reversed automatically within 5 to 7 working days.

## Charged twice
If you were charged twice for one order, contact support with your order number and payment screenshot. A billing team member reviews it and the extra amount is refunded.

## Wrong amount collected
If the courier collected a different amount from the order total, contact support right away with your order number. A billing team member investigates.

## Security
ShopEase staff and this assistant never ask for your card PIN, CVV, OTP codes or passwords. Never share them in chat.
