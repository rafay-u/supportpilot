# Policy edge cases

## Return requested after 7 days
If more than 7 days have passed since delivery, the item cannot be returned. If the item is defective, it may still be covered by warranty, so the customer can make a warranty claim.

## Damaged item without photos
A damaged item claim cannot be approved without photos of the item and packaging. Ask the customer to send clear photos. If they cannot, pass the case to a support team member.

## Damaged item reported after 48 hours
A damaged item reported after 48 hours but within 7 days of delivery is reviewed by a support team member. Do not approve or reject it yourself.

## One item damaged in a multi-item order
Only the damaged item is returned. The refund covers that item's price and nothing is deducted.

## Customer refused a cash on delivery parcel
The order is treated as cancelled and returned to origin. Nothing is owed. Repeated refusals can lead to cash on delivery being switched off.

## Address change after shipping
The address cannot be changed once the order has shipped. Suggest contacting the courier when they call, or refusing delivery if the order is no longer wanted. Offer to pass the case to the support team.

## Cancel request after shipping
The order cannot be cancelled. Explain the options: refuse delivery or return the item within 7 days if it is eligible.

## Price drop after buying
We do not adjust prices after purchase.

## Compensation for a late or bad experience
The assistant cannot promise compensation. It apologises and passes the case to the support team for review.

## Customer says a staff member promised something
The assistant cannot confirm promises made outside this chat. It passes the case to the support team.

## Asking about someone else's order
The assistant cannot discuss orders that belong to other customers. It tells the customer it can only help with their own orders.

## Wants a human
Pass the customer to a human immediately, whatever the topic.

## Legal threats or chargebacks
Pass the case to a human immediately and do not argue.

## Unsure or missing information
If the policy documents do not answer the question, say so and offer to have a team member confirm. Never invent a policy, price or deadline.
