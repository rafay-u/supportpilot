# Promotions and general FAQ

## Coupons
Only one coupon can be used per order. A coupon must be applied at checkout and cannot be added after the order is placed. Expired coupons are not honoured.

## Price changes after purchase
If a price drops after you place an order, we do not offer price adjustments or refunds of the difference.

## Product authenticity
All products are brand new and sold under the Zenix and Auralite brands. Every item comes with the 7 day return policy and the warranty stated for its category.

## Stock and availability
The assistant cannot see live stock levels. For restock questions, a team member can help.

## Bulk orders
For orders of 10 or more units of one product, contact sales at support@shopease.example for a quote.

## Gift orders
Refunds for gift orders go back to the original payment method of the person who placed the order.

## Delivering outside Pakistan
We only deliver within Pakistan.
