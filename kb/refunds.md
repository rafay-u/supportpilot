# Refunds

## When a refund is issued
A refund is issued after the returned item reaches our warehouse and passes inspection, which takes 1 to 2 working days. Refunds for cancelled orders that were already paid are issued as soon as the cancellation is confirmed.

## Refund amount
You are refunded the price of the returned items. For change of mind returns, PKR 250 return shipping is deducted. For damaged, wrong or defective items, nothing is deducted.

## Refund methods and timing
Card payments are refunded to the original card within 7 to 10 working days. Easypaisa and JazzCash payments are refunded to the same wallet within 3 to 5 working days. Cash on delivery orders are refunded to a bank account or mobile wallet that you provide, within 3 to 5 working days. We never refund cash in person.

## Refund details for cash on delivery orders
For a cash on delivery order we need your account title and either your IBAN or your Easypaisa or JazzCash wallet number. A support team member collects these details. Never share card PINs, OTP codes or passwords in chat.

## Larger refunds
Some refunds need a quick review by our team before they are approved. This can add up to 1 working day. You will be told when your refund is approved.

## Refund not received
If your refund has not arrived within the stated time, contact support with your order number. A team member checks the payment with our finance team.
