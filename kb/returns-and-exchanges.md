# Returns and exchanges

## Return window
You can return an item within 7 days of the delivery date. After 7 days we cannot accept a return, but a defective item may still be covered by warranty.

## Condition of returned items
Returned items must be unused, in the original packaging, with all accessories, manuals and the invoice. Items that show signs of use, damage caused by the customer, or missing parts can be refused.

## Free returns
Returns are free of charge when the item arrived damaged, is the wrong item, is defective, or is not as described. We arrange the pickup at no cost.

## Change of mind returns
If you simply changed your mind, you can return an eligible item within 7 days. A return shipping fee of PKR 250 is deducted from your refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Screen protectors are final sale and cannot be returned for change of mind, because they cannot be resold once delivered. If a screen protector arrives damaged or is the wrong item, report it using the damaged item steps below.

## Damaged or wrong items
Report a damaged, defective or wrong item within 48 hours of delivery, with clear photos of the item and the packaging. If it is reported after 48 hours but within the 7 day window, a support team member reviews it case by case. Without photos we cannot approve a damaged item claim, so we will ask you to send them.

## How to start a return
Send your order number and the reason for the return. We create a return request and, once approved, schedule a pickup within 1 to 2 working days. Your refund is processed after the item reaches our warehouse and is inspected.

## Exchanges
We can exchange an item for the same product or another item of the same price, subject to stock. If the replacement is not in stock, we refund the item instead.

## Partial returns
In an order with several items you can return just one item. You are refunded the price of that item only.
