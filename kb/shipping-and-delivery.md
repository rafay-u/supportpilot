# Shipping and delivery

## Delivery times
ShopEase delivers across Pakistan. Orders to Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad and Rawalpindi usually arrive 2 to 4 working days after the order ships. Other cities take 3 to 6 working days. Remote areas can take up to 8 working days. Working days are Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays.

## Processing time
An order is packed within 24 hours of being placed and handed to the courier within 1 to 2 working days. Delivery time starts counting from the day the order ships, not the day it is placed.

## Delivery charges
Delivery is free on all orders anywhere in Pakistan. We do not deliver outside Pakistan.

## Tracking
A tracking number is added to your order once it ships. The courier updates tracking about once every 24 hours, so a status can look unchanged for a day. When the order is out for delivery, it will arrive the same day and the courier calls before arriving.

## Delivery attempts
The courier tries up to 3 times and calls before each attempt. After 3 failed attempts the order returns to our warehouse and is cancelled.

## Late deliveries
If an order has been shipped for more than 7 working days and has not arrived, our support team opens an investigation with the courier. If the courier confirms the parcel is lost, or 12 working days pass after shipping with no delivery, the customer can choose a free replacement or a full refund. A support team member confirms the next step.

## Delivered but not received
If tracking says delivered but you did not receive the parcel, contact support within 3 days. A team member investigates with the courier before any replacement or refund is decided.
