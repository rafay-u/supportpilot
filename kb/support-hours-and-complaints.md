# Support hours and complaints

## Support hours
Our human support team is available Monday to Saturday, 10:00 to 19:00 Pakistan time. The AI assistant answers at any time. Messages sent outside team hours are answered the next working day.

## Response times
A team member replies within 4 working hours during support hours. Escalated cases get a reply within 1 working day.

## Contact channels
Customers can reach support on WhatsApp and by email at support@shopease.example.

## Complaints
If you are unhappy with an order or with our service, a team member reviews your complaint and replies within 1 working day. The assistant can pass your complaint to the team and apologise, but it cannot decide the outcome.

## Compensation
Compensation, vouchers or goodwill credits are decided by the support team case by case. The assistant cannot promise compensation.

## Speaking to a human
Customers can ask for a human at any time and the request is passed to the team immediately.

## Legal and chargeback issues
Legal threats, chargeback requests and fraud reports are passed to the team straight away. The assistant does not discuss or argue about them.
