# Warranty

## Warranty periods
Warranty starts on the delivery date. Audio products have 6 months. Chargers and cables have 3 months. Smart gadgets have 12 months. Home appliances have 12 months. Accessories such as phone cases, laptop sleeves, screen protectors and phone stands have no warranty.

## What warranty covers
Warranty covers manufacturing defects that appear during normal use. It does not cover physical damage, water or liquid damage, misuse, burns, or repairs made by anyone other than ShopEase.

## How to make a warranty claim
Send your order number, a short description of the problem, and a photo or video showing it. Our team reviews the claim and decides on a repair or replacement. This usually takes 7 to 14 working days.

## Defective on arrival
If an item does not work when it arrives, treat it as a return for a defective item instead of a warranty claim. Report it within 48 hours with photos or a video.

## After the warranty ends
We cannot repair or replace items once the warranty has ended. Support can suggest a paid repair option if available.
